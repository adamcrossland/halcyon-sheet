HALCYON · Runner's Files (macOS)
================================

To start
--------
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it (for example, Applications or Documents).
2. Right-click "Start Halcyon.command" and choose Open. macOS warns that it's from an
   unidentified developer; click Open. (You only need to right-click the first time.
   After that, double-click works.)
3. A Terminal window opens and your browser opens on the app.

To stop, close the Terminal window.

Your runners are saved in ~/Library/Application Support/HalcyonSheet/characters
(they stay there when you update the app).

If it won't start
-----------------
Open Terminal, drag this folder onto it after typing "cd ", press Return, then run:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine .
    codesign --force --sign - ./Halcyon
    ./Halcyon

Then open the address it prints (usually http://127.0.0.1:5180).

Which download?
---------------
- Halcyon-mac-apple-silicon: Macs with an M1, M2, M3 or later chip.
- Halcyon-mac-intel: older Intel Macs.
(Apple menu > About This Mac shows which one you have.)
