HALCYON · Runner's Files (Windows)
==================================

To start
--------
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it (for example, Documents).
2. Double-click Halcyon.exe.
   If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (The app isn't signed with a paid certificate.)
3. A console window opens and your browser opens on the app.

To stop, close the console window.

Your runners are saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\HalcyonSheet\characters
(they stay there when you update the app).

If the browser doesn't open, open the address shown in the console window
(usually http://127.0.0.1:5180).
